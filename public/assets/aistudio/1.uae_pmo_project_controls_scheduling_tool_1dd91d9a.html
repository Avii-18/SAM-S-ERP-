Climate, Ramadan & Grade Factors

Sharjah Approval Sequence (modelled in WBS 1.0 / 10.0)

  • Sharjah Municipality – building permit and final Completion Certificate (CCC).
  • Sharjah Civil Defence – fire & life-safety design approval, then final inspection before completion.
  • SEWA – electricity / water / gas NOCs, temporary power, and permanent connections (WBS 9.0 / 7.0).
  • Telecom (Etisalat / du), district-cooling provider where applicable, and Sharjah Urban Planning Council for zoning / master-plan items.
  • Authority durations are allowances only – confirm current procedures and fees with each authority.

UAE Working Calendar Rules

  • Midday outdoor-work break (12:30–15:00) applies 15 June – 15 September (MOHRE) – drives the summer productivity loss.
  • Ramadan: working hours reduced (typically 2 h/day) – drives the Ramadan loss factor.
  • Fixed holidays: New Year (1 Jan), National Day (2–3 Dec). Eid Al-Fitr, Arafat Day / Eid Al-Adha and Hijri New Year follow moon-sighting – add the officially announced dates in P6 before baselining.
  • Sites usually run Mon–Sat (6 days). Sharjah government offices run a Mon–Thu week, so allow for authority-response lag on Fridays.
  • Hot-weather concreting (temperature limits, retarders, night pours) is assumed inside the structure cycle times.
